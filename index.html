01 // About

Programmer first. Director second.

Shafi Chowdhury is the Managing Director of Shafi Consultancy Limited, a contract research organisation that specialises in statistical programming for pharmaceutical and CRO clients across all phases of drug development.

He has been presenting at industry conferences since 2005, on subjects from NONMEM dataset creation and good programming practice to risk-based monitoring, fraud detection and reporting driven entirely by metadata. Several of those papers were written with collaborators at sponsor companies.

The company works from Uxbridge in the United Kingdom and Sylhet in Bangladesh, where he has built a team of statistical programmers and statisticians.
